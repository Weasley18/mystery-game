# Design System: The Docket
**Project ID:** 8902697421525970392

## 1. Visual Theme & Atmosphere
Theatrical noir courtroom. High-contrast legal periodical on deep ink. Gravitas, archival evidence, simmering tension. No generic SaaS softness.

## 2. Color Palette & Roles
- Courtroom Ink (`#0c0e13`) — page void
- Burnished Oak (`#1a1b21` / `#1e1f25`) — cards and rails
- Verdict Crimson (`#c23b22`) — primary actions, prosecution accent, YES
- Aged Brass (`#ecc246` / `#c9a227`) — seals, defense accent, outlines
- Parchment (`#f4ede1` / `#eae1da`) — juror notes and verdict sheet
- Dust (`#a89a88` / `#e1bfb8`) — metadata labels
- On-surface (`#e2e2e9`) — body text on dark

## 3. Typography
- Headlines: Playfair Display, tight tracking, newspaper masthead
- Body / UI: Source Sans 3
- Labels: Source Sans 3, uppercase, wide letter-spacing (stamped docket)

## 4. Component Stylings
- **Buttons:** 8px corners. Primary filled crimson. Secondary brass outline, transparent fill.
- **Cards:** 12px corners, 1px brass at 30% (100% when selected). No drop shadows; inner 0.5px highlight.
- **Inputs:** Ink wells with brass underline / thin brass border.
- **Transcript slips:** Prosecution = brass left rail. Defense = slate rail. Juror = parchment card.

## 5. Layout Principles
12-column cinematic grid, generous margins, 4px baseline. Negative space is the courtroom ink.
